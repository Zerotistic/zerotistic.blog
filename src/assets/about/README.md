## Black-cat sticker

Final asset: [black-cat.png](black-cat.png). Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, not the CLI. The original RGBA output is preserved, including its alpha channel. It is a decorative illustration, not a claimed likeness or photo of the owner's pet.

### Final prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: small transparent decorative sticker for a personal anime-and-manga favorites shelf on a website.
Primary request: one adorable black house cat sleeping curled up, paws tucked under its face and tail wrapped around its body.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background, clean alpha, no ground plane, no checkerboard painted into the image.
Style/medium: polished hand-drawn anime illustration, gentle ink contours and soft charcoal shading, a little fur texture, sweet understated expression. It should sit naturally beside an existing anime character illustration.
Composition/framing: single complete cat, wide compact silhouette, centered with a small clear margin. Both ears and the entire curled tail visible. Readable at approximately 100px wide.
Color palette: black and charcoal fur, subtle cool grey highlights, restrained warm inner ears. A fine warm off-white sticker edge for legibility on both white and near-black web backgrounds.
Constraints: only the cat; no accessories, collar, text, letters, logos, hearts, stars, extra animals, furniture, background or heavy shadow. This is a generic illustration, not a portrait of a particular real pet.
```
